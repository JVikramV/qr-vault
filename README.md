QR Vault 🔐

QR Vault is a Flutter application that automatically discovers, organizes, and manages payment QR codes stored in your device gallery.

Instead of searching through hundreds of screenshots to find a payment QR, QR Vault creates a dedicated wallet for all your payment QR codes.

⸻

✨ Features

🔍 Automatic QR Discovery

* Scans your gallery for QR codes
* Detects payment QR screenshots automatically
* Processes images using Google ML Kit

💳 UPI QR Extraction

* Extracts merchant names
* Extracts UPI IDs
* Stores payment information in a clean, organized format

⭐ Favorites

* Mark frequently used payment QRs as favorites
* Favorites appear at the top of the list
* Favorites are stored permanently using local storage

🔄 Duplicate Detection

* Automatically removes duplicate QR entries
* Prevents the same payment QR from appearing multiple times

🔎 Smart Search

* Search by merchant name
* Search by UPI ID
* Instant filtering of results

📱 QR Detail View

* View QR codes in full-screen mode
* Display merchant information clearly
* Easy access to payment actions

💸 Pay With…

* Launch supported UPI payment applications
* Compatible with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and other UPI apps

📋 Copy UPI ID

* Copy UPI IDs to clipboard with a single tap

📤 Share QR

* Share payment QR screenshots directly
* Share merchant details and QR image through any supported app

🎨 Modern UI

* Custom QR Vault branding
* Dark purple fintech-inspired theme
* Custom application icon
* Smooth user experience
